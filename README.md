Privacy Policy for Jaunty
Last updated: September 15, 2026

Jaunty is designed with privacy in mind. The app operates locally on your device and does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to external servers.
1. Data Collection and Storage
 All course details, timetable entries, and preferences are stored exclusively on your device.
 No user tracking across apps or websites. No advertising or user profiling frameworks.
2. Device Permissions
 Location: Used strictly on-device via Apple MapKit to calculate travel times and transit routes to campus. Coordinates are never logged, stored, or transmitted off your device.
 Notifications: Scheduled locally on your device solely to alert you before upcoming classes. No remote push notifications or remote servers are involved.
3. Third-Party Services
Jaunty contains no third-party analytics SDKs, tracking libraries, or ad networks. Network requests are limited to native Apple services handled directly by iOS (such as MapKit routing).
4. Data Retention and Deletion
All data remains exclusively on your device. Deleting Jaunty immediately and permanently erases all stored schedules, course records, and local settings.
